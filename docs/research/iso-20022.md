# ISO 20022 Research Notes

## Purpose

Track ISO 20022 concepts that may inform future payment, settlement, and reporting models.

## Overview

ISO 20022 is a financial messaging standard. pay-orchestration should not copy the standard wholesale, but should learn from its vocabulary around parties, accounts, agents, payment status, remittance, and settlement.

## Concepts to Study

- Party and agent modeling.
- Account identification.
- Payment initiation messages.
- Payment status reports.
- Remittance information.
- Settlement method and clearing system identifiers.

## Relevance to pay-orchestration

- May improve naming for Party, Asset Account, and Settlement concepts.
- May guide status evidence mapping from bank rails.
- May help future reporting and reconciliation language.

## Future Improvements

- Map ISO 20022 party/account terms to ontology terms.
- Collect examples from payout and settlement messages.
- Identify which concepts are too banking-specific for the core domain.

## Open Questions

- Which ISO 20022 concepts are useful without overfitting to bank rails?
- Should future provider adapters normalize some evidence using ISO 20022-inspired language?
- How should remittance data be represented?
