# FIBO Research Notes

## Purpose

Track lessons from the Financial Industry Business Ontology without adopting it as the project model.

## Overview

FIBO provides formal financial concepts for contracts, parties, instruments, and financial relationships. pay-orchestration can use it as a reference for precision, not as a dependency.

## Concepts to Study

- Legal entity and party distinctions.
- Financial account concepts.
- Obligation and contract language.
- Currency and instrument representation.
- Transaction and event modeling.

## Relevance to pay-orchestration

- Can sharpen Party and Tenant definitions.
- Can inform Asset and Asset Account modeling.
- Can help avoid casual language around obligations and settlement.

## Future Improvements

- Create a term comparison table between FIBO and pay-orchestration.
- Identify concepts worth borrowing.
- Identify concepts intentionally out of scope.

## Open Questions

- Is formal ontology useful for this project, or too heavy for implementation?
- Which legal terms must be avoided until compliance scope is understood?
- Should the project maintain a lightweight ontology separate from implementation models?
